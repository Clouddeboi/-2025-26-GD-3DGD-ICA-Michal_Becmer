# Game Name

The Cigar Room

# Overview

Write a short description of your escape room experience.

You play as a journalist in the 1980s investigating the town’s notorious mafia boss in search of a major story. After sneaking into his mansion, you become trapped inside a mysterious cigar room filled with hidden clues and secrets.

To escape, you must explore the environment, uncover concealed information, and solve a series of interconnected puzzles in order to discover the code needed to unlock the door.

# How to Play

Movement: WASD

Look around: Mouse

Interact: E key

Examine: Scroll Wheel (zoom in/out), Left click drag (Rotate object)

Equip Flashlight: 1 Key

Turn on Flashlight: Left Mouse Click

# Gameplay loop:

- Explore the room
- Examine clues
- Collect useful items
- Use information/items to modify the environment
- Solve the puzzle and escape

# WARNING: SPOILERS BELOW
<details>
Puzzle 1 - Sofa Puzzle
  
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
  
Step 1: Navigate to the right side of the room (looking at windows), on top of the shelves, you will see an item out of reach

Step 2: Interact with sofa to push it toward the shelf

Step 3: Walk ontop of sofa

Step 4: Now the player can collect the Item (Pasta Box)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  

Puzzle 2 - Chandelier Puzzle

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
  
Step 1: Navigate to the left side of the room (looking at windows), on the wall, there will be a slot to insert an item into an item frame

Step 2: Interact with the item frame, it will take the Pasta Box and place it inside

Step 3: The Chandelier will start to lower behind the player

Step 4: Now the player can collect the Key that was previously inaccesible

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  

Puzzle 3 - Flashlight Puzzle

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
  
Step 1: Navigate to the left side of the room (looking at windows), there will be a box on a stool

Step 2: Interact with the box, it will take the key from the player's inventory and open the box

Step 3: Inside there is a flashlight you can pickup, equip it with 1 and activate it with Left mouse click

Step 4: Turn off the lights with the light switch

Step 5: Shine the light on the painting on the wall, this will reveal the code

Step 6: Input the code on the padlock on the exit door

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
